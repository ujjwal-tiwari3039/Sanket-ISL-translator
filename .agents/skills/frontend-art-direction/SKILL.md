---
name: frontend-art-direction
description: Establish a new visual direction or redesign the composition of a Sanket landing page. Not for routine component fixes.
---

Read DESIGN.md and inspect current rendered pages before editing. Identify audience, primary action and verified product claims. Propose a composition with one memorable visual motif, a type hierarchy, grid, whitespace rhythm and restrained palette. Describe each section's role before building it. Prefer editorial columns and continuous surfaces to interchangeable card grids. Avoid ornamental gradients, pills, random icons and oversized type without hierarchy. Preserve the recognizer, camera lifecycle and demo separation. Render the highest-impact section first and refine before extending the direction.
