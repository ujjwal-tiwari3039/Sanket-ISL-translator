---
name: image-to-ui
description: Analyze a supplied image, screenshot, video or website reference and reinterpret its visual principles for Sanket. Not for literal cloning or tasks without references.
---

Inspect the actual reference; for video sample frames across its duration and disclose if audio is not reviewed. Identify composition, grid, typography, palette, spacing, image role, interaction and implied motion. Separate reusable principles from source-specific identity and content. Reinterpret for ISL and the product's real capabilities. Document source and adaptations in docs/design/audit.md. Do not copy proprietary code, assets or skill text. If a reference cannot be accessed, say so and work from available evidence. Validate the interpretation against a rendered page.
