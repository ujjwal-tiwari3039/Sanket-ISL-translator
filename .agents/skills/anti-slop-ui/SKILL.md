---
name: anti-slop-ui
description: Critique a UI that feels generic, templated or visually incoherent; propose concrete composition corrections. Not for backend or functional-only bugs.
---

Compare the rendered page with DESIGN.md. Ask whether its sections could belong to hundreds of unrelated products, whether cards hide weak hierarchy, whether typography and spacing carry meaning, and whether gradients or motion substitute for identity. Locate the strongest and weakest regions. Replace repetitive patterns with a project-specific composition: landmark notation, meaningful whitespace, editorial type and a clear action. Prefer fewer stronger components. Preserve useful content. Explain each change in terms of hierarchy or product meaning; do not replace one fashionable template with another.
